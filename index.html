Pipeline

Four modules. One accountable output.

The novelty is not a bigger network. It is a per-pixel evidence map that travels with every super-resolved image.

M1

Evidence Harvester

Collects multiple Sentinel-2 passes plus Sentinel-1 radar over the same area, and records which passes were clear or blocked.

M2

Multi-Frame SR

Fuses the passes into one 4× output, from 10 m to 2.5 m, using real sub-pixel differences between acquisitions.

M3

Truth Auditor

Scores each pixel using consistency residual, ensemble disagreement and radar edge agreement.

M4

Analyst Console

Roads, buildings, change detection and phantom flags, each traceable to the dated passes behind it.

Evidence map

Every pixel says how much to trust it.

A normal super-resolution model can invent a convincing building that was never there. Ábyssos labels that risk instead of hiding it.

Design choice

Why 4×, and not 20×.

A 10 m pixel cannot hold 0.5 m information, so we choose 4×. Scale is limited by what the passes actually observed
